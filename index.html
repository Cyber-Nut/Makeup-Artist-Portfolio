HD or airbrush, which one should I pick?

HD looks closer to real skin and works for most brides. Airbrush is lighter and lasts longer in heat or for very long days. We'll pick on the call based on your skin and the venue.

Will the makeup last through the whole night?

The base is set for 12+ hours and tested under camera flash before you leave the chair. A small touch-up kit stays with you for lipstick and blotting.
